Core Architecture

Engineered for Academic & Enterprise Rigor

Combining state-of-the-art natural language processing with automated quantitative data profiling.

T5 Deep Learning Model

Employs Google's Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer (T5) trained on vast linguistic datasets to generate coherent, fluent abstractive summaries.

PyMuPDF High-Fidelity Parser

Extracts structured text, embedded metadata, and multi-page chapters from dense academic PDFs with precision and formatting preservation.

Leverages Pandas and openpyxl to analyze Excel and CSV spreadsheets, generating metric distributions, averages, and cohort summaries automatically.
