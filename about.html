About BriefIQ

AI Summary and Report Generator Using Deep Learning – Engineered to simplify document summarization and dataset analysis through cutting-edge neural models.

Overview & Mission

BriefIQ is a unified web-based application designed to bridge the gap between extensive textual information and dense structured numerical datasets. In today's digital era, researchers, educators, students, and corporate analysts are overwhelmed with voluminous PDF research papers, institutional textbooks, and multifaceted Excel spreadsheets.

By integrating deep learning sequence-to-sequence transformers, optical text extraction engines, and automated exploratory data analysis, BriefIQ eliminates manual reading bottlenecks, slashes cognitive fatigue, and surfaces crucial insights in a fraction of a second.

T5 Deep Learning Model

BriefIQ leverages Google's T5-small (Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer). T5 reframes all NLP tasks into a unified text-to-text format, utilizing encoder-decoder self-attention to generate fluent, contextually accurate abstractive summaries rather than simply copying raw sentences.

HuggingFace Transformers PyTorch Runtime SentencePiece

PyMuPDF & Pandas Engine

Text extraction is powered by PyMuPDF (fitz), capable of extracting text across hundreds of pages in milliseconds. Structured datasets from CSV, TSV, and Excel (XLSX, XLS) are parsed using Pandas and openpyxl to compute descriptive statistics, frequency counts, and anomalies.

PyMuPDF (fitz) Pandas 2.2+ OpenPyXL
